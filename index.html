I am a Foundation AI Engineer at VinRobotics, working on Vision-Language-Action models and Imitation Learning, advised by Dr. An Thai Le and Dr. Ngo Anh Vien. I obtained my B.Sc. degree in Computer Science (Advanced Program in Computer Science) from the University of Science, Viet Nam National University Ho Chi Minh City (HCMUS) in 2024, advised by Prof. Dien Dinh and Dr. Long Nguyen.
